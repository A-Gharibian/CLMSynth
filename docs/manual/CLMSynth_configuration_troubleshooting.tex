\vspace{0.2em}

\noindent This reference documents every configuration condition that is either an incompatibility or an ignored setting, as well as the diagnostic message of the CLMSynth label engine. It is intended to help users \emph{plan} a valid configuration and \emph{interpret} what the engine does with a specific configuration.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{How the code works.} The identifiers below are a documentation index for this manual; the core label synthesizer engine raises standard Python exceptions with messages banded by type:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\vcode{1xx} & \textbf{ValueError} invalid value (\vcode{100}--\vcode{110}) or\\
 & \textbf{Incompatible} option (\vcode{111}--\vcode{149}) or \\
 & \textbf{InfeasibleAllocationError}, Infeasible outcome (\vcode{150}--\vcode{169}). \\
\kcode{2xx} & \textbf{KeyError} a required configuration key is missing (message is just the key name). \\
\wcode{3xx} & \textbf{Warning} non-fatal; logged via \texttt{logging.warning}, execution continues. \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\quad Requirements}{Requirements} (planning)}
The user needs to provide, through a configuration file, a required set of configuration settings (Table~2). It is necessary to plan the required configuration before executing the label synthesizer; an independent CLI wizard module (\texttt{config\_wizard}) provides step-by-step generation of the file.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{4.2cm} L{12.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
\headrow \hc{Setting} & \hc{Requires} \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\headrow \hc{Setting} & \hc{Requires} \\ \midrule
\endhead
\texttt{num\_classes} ($M$) & Integer in $[1,64]$ ($\geq 2$ under \texttt{single}); the dataset's own cluster count $K$ is capped the same way. A coarse safety backstop (\vcode{126}/\vcode{127}), not a validated reliability boundary, internal testing already finds results unreliable well below this, at $K\gtrsim15$ or $M\gtrsim20$. \\
\texttt{matching\_mode} & One of \texttt{perfect}, \texttt{single}, \texttt{random}, \texttt{custom}. \\
\texttt{balance: unbalanced} & Either explicit \texttt{proportions} \emph{or} a \texttt{skew\_rule}. Explicit proportions need exactly \texttt{num\_classes} entries (\vcode{121}) summing to $1$ (\vcode{106}). Rules have defaults, so \texttt{skew\_params} may be omitted. \\
\texttt{skew\_params} & \texttt{skew\_params} for its type: \texttt{geometric}$\to$\texttt{ratio}; \texttt{dominant\_minority}$\to$\texttt{dominant\_index},\texttt{dominant\_share}; \texttt{dirichlet}$\to$\texttt{alpha}. \\
\texttt{matching\_mode:}\\
\texttt{perfect} & \texttt{num\_classes} $==$ number of clusters ($M=K$); a bijection needs one label per cluster.\\
\texttt{single} & $M\geq2$, $K\geq2$, and \texttt{single\_match:\{cluster,label\}}; the cluster must exist and the label must be in $0..M{-}1$. Refer to \texttt{target\_metric.scope} \\
\texttt{custom} & \texttt{assignment\_matrix} with $\geq1$ row; each row's \texttt{label}$\in 0..M{-}1$ and \texttt{clusters} exist; each row needs \texttt{recall\_target} \emph{unless} \texttt{target\_metric} is set. \\
\texttt{target\_metric} & \texttt{matching\_mode} $\in$ \{\texttt{single}, \texttt{custom}\}; \texttt{type}$\in$\{\texttt{mcc},\texttt{ari}\}; \texttt{value}$\in[-1,1]$. \\
\texttt{target\_metric.scope} & \texttt{"global"} (default) or \texttt{"pair"}. \texttt{pair} additionally requires \texttt{type: mcc} and \texttt{matching\_mode: single}, and is met in closed form (\texttt{tolerance}/\texttt{max\_iter} are not read). \\
spatial placement & Per-point feature vectors (an $(N,d)$ \texttt{coords} array) whenever \texttt{centroid\_dependence} is enabled or a \texttt{competing\_noise} entry favors \texttt{core}/\texttt{boundary}; labels-only configs must omit them. \\
\texttt{competing\_noise} & \texttt{matching\_mode} $\in$ \{\texttt{single}, \texttt{custom}\}; each entry: \texttt{cluster} exists, \texttt{label}$\in 0..M{-}1$, \texttt{share}$\in[0,1]$, \texttt{favors}$\in$\{\texttt{core},\texttt{boundary},\texttt{random}\}. Needs \emph{unclaimed} (leftover) points to have any effect. \\
\texttt{centroid\_dependence} & \texttt{profile}$\in$\{\texttt{linear},\texttt{exponential},\texttt{step}\}; \texttt{favors}$\in$\{\texttt{core},\texttt{boundary}\}, matched exactly and case-sensitively (\vcode{129}); \texttt{steepness} only for \texttt{exponential}. \\
\texttt{concentrated\_labels} & Under \texttt{spillover\_rule: concentrated} only: a \emph{list} of integer label ids, each $\in 0..M{-}1$ (\vcode{128}). A bare number is not a shorthand for a one-element list. Omit the key to take the default, the single largest label. \\
\bottomrule
\caption{List of configuration a user is required to plan and provide to the software}
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Incompatibilities}
Incompatibilities are configurations in which every individual setting is valid, but the combination is not: the requested features are mutually exclusive so that no assignment can satisfy both at once.
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{3.4cm} L{4.6cm} L{1.2cm} L{6.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
\headrow \hc{Feature} & \hc{Not allowed when} & \hc{Code} & \hc{Reason \& fix} \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\headrow \hc{Feature} & \hc{Not allowed when} & \hc{Code} & \hc{Reason \& fix} \\ \midrule
\endhead
\texttt{target\_metric} & \texttt{matching\_mode = perfect} & \vcode{111} & Perfect is a fixed bijection (MCC$=1$); there is no free \texttt{recall\_target} to search. Use \texttt{single}/\texttt{custom}, or drop \texttt{target\_metric}. \\
\texttt{target\_metric} & \texttt{matching\_mode = random} & \vcode{114} & Random ignores clusters entirely; there is no agreement level to dial. Use \texttt{single}/\texttt{custom}. \\
\texttt{competing\_noise} & \texttt{matching\_mode = random} & \vcode{115} & There is no aligned label for a competing label to compete with. Use \texttt{single}/\texttt{custom}. \\
\texttt{target\_metric.}\newline \texttt{scope: pair} & \texttt{type = ari} & \vcode{123} & The single-pair MCC inverts in closed form; ARI has no such inverse. Use \texttt{type: mcc}, or \texttt{scope: global}. \\
\texttt{target\_metric.}\newline \texttt{scope: pair} & \texttt{matching\_mode} $\neq$ \texttt{single} & \vcode{124} & The target pair is the \texttt{single\_match} (cluster, label). Use \texttt{scope: global} for \texttt{custom}. \\
\texttt{spatial placement} & \texttt{features} missing or empty & \vcode{125} & Centroid distances need complete feature values. Pass an $(N,d)$ feature array, or disable \texttt{centroid\_dependence}. \\
\texttt{centroid\_\newline dependence.\newline favors} & not \texttt{core}/\texttt{boundary} & \vcode{129} &  Matching is case-sensitive and exact. Any other value, including a capitalization slip (\texttt{Core}) or a typo (\texttt{boundary}), is only read when \texttt{enabled: true}. \\
\texttt{target\_metric.}\newline \texttt{scope: pair} & \texttt{spillover\_rule} is \texttt{uniform}; or \texttt{concentrated} targeting the pair's own label (or left to its default); or a \texttt{competing\_noise} entry emitting that label & \vcode{130} & The closed form sizes $\ell^*$ so that \emph{all} of it sits inside $k^*$, and there is no search to correct a miss. Anything that can emit $\ell^*$ elsewhere silently delivers a different coefficient. Use \texttt{proportional\_to\_marginal}, whose leftover pool for $\ell^*$ is empty by construction, or \texttt{scope: global}. \\
\bottomrule
\caption{Incompatibilities between several configurations, which are reported as 1xx value errors as invalid configuration, but cannot be diagnosed by inspecting any single field.}
\end{longtable}

\section{Ignored \& Irrelevant Settings (expectations)}
% ===========================================================================
If the requirements are met with no incompatibilities in configuration (Section~4.1--4.3), all other mode$\times$option mismatches are not considered as errors and are ignored with a warning (see Section~4.4 for a complete reference).

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{4.2cm} L{5.4cm} L{6.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
\headrow \hc{Setting} & \hc{Ignored / irrelevant when} & \hc{Effect} \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\headrow \hc{Setting} & \hc{Ignored / irrelevant when} & \hc{Effect} \\ \midrule
\endhead
\texttt{proportions} & \texttt{balance = balanced} & Replaced by a uniform $1/M$ split. Warns \wcode{301}. \\
\texttt{proportions}, \texttt{balance}, \texttt{skew\_rule} & \texttt{matching\_mode = perfect} & Label counts are forced to each paired cluster's size. Warns \wcode{302}. \\
\texttt{skew\_rule} & \texttt{balance = unbalanced} \emph{and} \texttt{proportions} given & Explicit \texttt{proportions} take precedence; \texttt{skew\_rule} is the fallback only. \\
per-row \texttt{recall\_target} & \texttt{target\_metric} is set & Overridden by the single solved global $\alpha$. Warns \wcode{303}. \\
\texttt{centroid\_dependence} & \texttt{matching\_mode = perfect} & Runs but has no visible effect: each cluster is $100\%$ one label, so placement cannot discriminate. \\
\texttt{centroid\_dependence} & \texttt{matching\_mode = random} & Placement never runs: random returns before the placement stage. The \emph{coords guard} still applies, though, being checked ahead of that early return: \texttt{enabled: true} without feature vectors raises \vcode{125} even here. \\
\texttt{competing\_noise} & \texttt{matching\_mode = perfect} & No-op: zero unclaimed capacity to convert. Warns \wcode{305} per entry. \\
\texttt{split\_rule} & a rule targets a single cluster & Short-circuited (the point mass goes to that one cluster directly). \\
\texttt{split\_rule}, \texttt{spillover\_rule} & \texttt{matching\_mode = random} & No per-cluster allocation step exists at all in random mode. \\
\texttt{single\_match} & \texttt{matching\_mode} $\neq$ \texttt{single} & Not read. \\
\texttt{assignment\_matrix} & \texttt{matching\_mode} $\neq$ \texttt{custom} & Not read. \\
\texttt{steepness} & \texttt{profile} $\neq$ \texttt{exponential} & Unused (only the exponential weight uses it). \\
\texttt{concentrated\_labels} & \texttt{spillover\_rule} $\neq$ \texttt{concentrated} & Unused, and not validated in this case; default target is the single largest label. Under \texttt{concentrated} it \emph{is} validated (\vcode{128}). \\
\texttt{tolerance}, \texttt{max\_iter} & \texttt{target\_metric.scope: pair} & Not read; the pair target is met in closed form, not by search. \\
\bottomrule
\caption{Ignored configurations and their effect.}
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Value Error (invalid configuration) \texorpdfstring{\vcode{1xx}}{1xx}}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{1.0cm} L{3.5cm} L{4.6cm} L{5.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
\headrow \hc{Code} & \hc{Trigger} & \hc{Reason} & \hc{Mitigation} \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\headrow \hc{Code} & \hc{Trigger} & \hc{Reason} & \hc{Mitigation} \\ \midrule
\endhead
\vcode{101} & \texttt{matching\_mode} is none of the four modes & Unrecognised mode string & Use \texttt{perfect}, \texttt{single}, \texttt{random}, or \texttt{custom}. \\
\vcode{102} & \texttt{perfect} with $M\neq K$ & A bijection needs exactly one label per cluster & Set \texttt{num\_classes} to the true cluster count, or switch to \texttt{custom}. \\
\vcode{103} & \texttt{single} with $M<2$ or $K<2$ & Single needs a target label \emph{and} at least one other, over $\geq2$ clusters & Raise \texttt{num\_classes}; use data with $\geq2$ clusters. \\
\vcode{104} & \texttt{single\_match.label} / a row \texttt{label} not an integer in $0..M{-}1$ & The label id does not exist & Use a value in $0..$\texttt{num\_classes}$-1$. \\
\vcode{105} & \texttt{single\_match.cluster} / a row \texttt{cluster} not among the data's ids & Typo, or wrong id \emph{type} (\texttt{byoc} ids are whatever your CSV column holds, and may be strings) & Use an id from the data; match the type (int vs string). \\
\vcode{106} & explicit \texttt{proportions} do not sum to $1$ (tol.\ $10^{-6}$) & Not a probability distribution & Rescale so the entries sum to $1$. \\
\vcode{107} & \texttt{skew\_rule} not in \{\texttt{geometric},\texttt{dominant\_minority},\texttt{dirichlet}\} & Unrecognised skew rule & Use one of the three, or give \texttt{proportions} directly. \\
\vcode{108} & \texttt{split\_rule} not in \{\texttt{proportional\_to\_size},\texttt{equal}\} & Unrecognised split rule & Use one of the two. \\
\vcode{109} & \texttt{spillover\_rule} not in \{\texttt{proportional\_to\_marginal},\texttt{uniform},\texttt{concentrated}\} & Unrecognised spillover rule & Use one of the three. \\
\vcode{110} & \texttt{profile} not in \{\texttt{linear},\texttt{exponential},\texttt{step}\} & Unrecognised placement profile & Use one of the three. \\
\hline
\vcode{111} & \texttt{target\_metric} under \texttt{perfect} & No free \texttt{recall\_target} to solve for & Use \texttt{single}/\texttt{custom}, or drop \texttt{target\_metric}. \\
\vcode{112} & \texttt{target\_metric.type} not \texttt{mcc}/\texttt{ari} & Only these two metrics are solvable & Set \texttt{type: mcc} or \texttt{ari}. \\
\vcode{113} & \texttt{target\_metric.value} outside $[-1,1]$ & MCC/ARI live in $[-1,1]$ & Choose a value in range. \\
\vcode{114} & \texttt{target\_metric} under \texttt{random} & No cluster--label structure to dial & Use \texttt{single}/\texttt{custom}. \\
\vcode{115} & \texttt{competing\_noise} under \texttt{random} & No aligned label to compete with & Use \texttt{single}/\texttt{custom}. \\
\vcode{116} & \texttt{competing\_noise.favors} not \texttt{core}/\texttt{boundary}/\texttt{random} & Unrecognised placement side & Use one of the three. \\
\vcode{117} & \texttt{competing\_noise.share} outside $[0,1]$ & Share is a fraction of leftover points & Choose \texttt{share} in $[0,1]$. \\
\vcode{118} & \texttt{competing\_noise.label} outside $0..M{-}1$ & The competing label id does not exist & Use a valid label id. \\
\vcode{119} & \texttt{competing\_noise.cluster} not among the data's ids &    \phantom{ } Typo or wrong id type & Use an existing cluster id. \\
\vcode{120} & \texttt{target\_metric}: every probed $\alpha\in[0,1]$ is infeasible & The rule clusters cannot hold any recall level (Table~\ref{IAE}) & Enlarge the target clusters, lower \texttt{num\_classes}, or adjust \texttt{proportions}. \\
\vcode{121} & \texttt{proportions} length $\neq$ \texttt{num\_classes} & A longer list silently enlarged the label space: the surplus entries became label ids that \texttt{num\_classes} never declared, written into the dataset through \texttt{uniform}/\texttt{concentrated} spillover. A shorter list left labels unallocated & provided exactly \texttt{num\_classes} proportions. Not raised under \texttt{balance: balanced}, where \texttt{proportions} are ignored outright (\wcode{301}). \\
\vcode{122} & \texttt{target\_metric.scope} not \texttt{pair}/\texttt{global} & Unrecognised scope string & Use \texttt{pair} or \texttt{global}. \\
\vcode{123} & \texttt{scope: pair} with \texttt{type: ari} & Only the single-pair MCC has a closed-form inverse & Use \texttt{type: mcc}, or \texttt{scope: global}. \\
\vcode{124} & \texttt{scope: pair} outside \texttt{single} mode & The pair is defined by \texttt{single\_match} & Use \texttt{matching\_mode: single}, or \texttt{scope: global}. \\
\vcode{125} & placement requested without feature vectors & Centroid distances need $(N,d)$ coordinates & Pass \texttt{coords}, or disable \texttt{centroid\_dependence} and spatial \texttt{competing\_noise}. \\
\vcode{126} & \texttt{num\_classes} outside $[1,64]$ & Coarse safety backstop against runaway/typo configs, not a validated reliability limit (see Table 2) & Use a value in $[1,64]$; results are already unreliable well before this ceiling. \\
\vcode{127} & the dataset's cluster count $K > 64$ & Same backstop, applied to the data rather than the config & Regroup or subsample clusters to $K\leq64$. \\
\vcode{128} & \texttt{concentrated\_labels} is not a list of integers in $0..M{-}1$ & The list is drawn from directly and written into the label column, so an unchecked value puts a label in the dataset that \texttt{num\_classes} never declared. A bare number is read as a \emph{range} (scattering the remainder over that many labels, the opposite of \texttt{concentrated}); a non-integer is truncated on write & Give a list of existing label ids, e.g. \texttt{[0]}. Checked before the \texttt{target\_metric} solver, so a solved score can never be computed over a label that does not exist. \\
\vcode{129} & \texttt{centroid\_dependence} mode \texttt{.favors} not \texttt{core}/\texttt{boundary} & Matched exactly and case-sensitively; any other value, including \texttt{Core} or a typo, was silently treated as \texttt{boundary} & Use lowercase \texttt{core} or \texttt{boundary}. Only read when \texttt{enabled: true}. \\
\vcode{130} & \texttt{scope: pair} combined with a spillover or noise setting that can place the target label outside its cluster & The closed-form inversion assumes every point of $\ell^*$ lies in $k^*$; \texttt{uniform} spillover, \texttt{concentrated} onto $\ell^*$, and \texttt{competing\_noise} emitting $\ell^*$ all break that assumption. & Use \texttt{spillover\_rule: proportional\_to\_marginal}, keep \texttt{competing\_noise} off $\ell^*$, or use \texttt{scope: global}. \\
\vcode{131} & \texttt{skew\_params} out of range for the chosen \texttt{skew\_rule} & Unchecked values mostly did not crash: they produced \emph{negative} label counts that still summed to $N$, so largest-remainder rounding was satisfied and the run delivered a labeling nobody requested. The remaining cases raised uncoded \texttt{ZeroDivisionError}/\texttt{IndexError} & Use \texttt{geometric} \texttt{ratio} $\geq 0$; \texttt{dominant\_minority} \texttt{dominant\_share} $\in[0,1]$ with integer \texttt{dominant\_index} $\in 0..M{-}1$ and $M\geq2$; \texttt{dirichlet} \texttt{alpha} $>0$. Only read when \texttt{balance} is not \texttt{balanced} \emph{and} no \texttt{proportions} are given; omit \texttt{skew\_params} for the defaults. \\
\bottomrule
\caption{Complete list of invalid configurations.}
\label{LIC}
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Uncoded Rejections (data import)}
% ===========================================================================

\noindent Not every rejection is a \vcode{CLM} diagnostic. The banded codes above describe the \emph{cluster-label matching model}: what a configuration asks the engine to do, and whether that is coherent. The conditions in this section describe something prior to it, whether the \emph{data} that was handed to the engine is a usable clustering at all.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent This section is expected to grow as more input paths gain checks. Currently, it covers one: \textbf{Bring-Your-Own-Clusters} (\texttt{data\_source: byoc}).

\subsubsection*{BYOC input requirements}

\noindent BYOC is an \emph{import} path, not a generator. The premise is that the user has already clustered a subset of their features with their own algorithm (k-means, fuzzy c-means, or any other) and is bringing the result: the feature subset in which the clustering was computed, plus one column holding each point's cluster. The requirements in the following are derived from that premise rather than from the CLM engine.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent Every requirement is checked in a single pass, so a file with several problems reports all of them at once rather than one per attempt. Any failure rejects that dataset; under a multi-dataset configuration, the remaining datasets are unaffected.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{What is \emph{not} restricted.} Cluster identifiers may be integers or strings, in any script; if \texttt{single\_match} or \texttt{assignment\_matrix} references them, the type must match. The names of the feature columns are carried verbatim to the output CSV and the plot axes. Repeated identical rows are kept, since they are additional points rather than a data error, and removing them would change the cluster sizes on which the matching arithmetic is built.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Known limitation.} There is currently no way to carry a column that is neither a feature nor the cluster, an additional label or ``other tag'' that should travel with the data without entering the geometry. Every non-cluster column is a feature, which is why all of them must be numeric. A declaration for such columns is planned.
